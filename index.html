Resistance is the language of liberation.

Palestinians have expressed resistance through rebuilding, education, testimony, documentation, sumud, boycott, art, collective refusal, protest, and writing.

Resistance does not take only one form. It preserves memory, protects identity, sustains community, challenges injustice, and imagines a liberated future.
